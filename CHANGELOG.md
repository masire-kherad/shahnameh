## [1.1.0] - 2026-02-17

### Added
- Initial release of MasireKherad (مسیر خرد)
- Gamified reading experience for Shahnameh poems
- Cross-platform support (iOS, Android, Web)
- Dark mode support
- Favorites and completed poems tracking
- Challenge mode (experimental) for Keyumars, Hooshang, and Tahmores sections
- Audio support for poems
- Persian (RTL) language support
- Progress tracking and achievements
